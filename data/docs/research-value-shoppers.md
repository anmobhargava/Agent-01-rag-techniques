# Consumer research: Value Shoppers

## Who they are
Price-conscious households, usually with children at home, who plan their shopping around promotions and unit prices. They buy in multipacks and compare shelf prices across stores.

## How they find products
They respond to retailer apps, loyalty offers, circulars and in-store signs. They rarely discover products on TikTok. A visible price on the ad matters more to them than the message.

## What they buy
Multipacks of Lunchbox Bites and family-size Original. They stock up when there is a deal and switch brands when the shelf price moves too far.

## Price sensitivity
This audience felt the April price increase most. Original volume with Value Shoppers fell 3.8% in the following quarter. Standard brand advertising did not recover them. Offers on multipacks inside retailer media did.

## Implication
Speak to Value Shoppers where they plan the shop, and lead with price and pack size.
