# Campaign: Leaf Local

## Brief
Leaf Local launched in April to introduce Leaf Crisps to Gen Z Snackers. Instead of a television spot, the team paid 60 TikTok creators to show how they snack during study sessions and road trips. Tidewater Creative managed the creators and the content calendar.

## Results
Spend was 6.2 million dollars and the return was 2.6 dollars per dollar, the best of any brand campaign in the first half. Leaf Crisps sales lifted 11% in the markets where creators were active. It also drove the most comments and saves of any Crunchwell content to date.

## Learnings
Creators who tried the product on camera before talking about it performed twice as well as those reading a script. Effects faded quickly when posting stopped, which is why the team later chose to keep creators on retainer. The campaign did almost nothing for older households.
