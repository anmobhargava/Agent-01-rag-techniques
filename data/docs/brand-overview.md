# Crunchwell Snacks: brand overview

Crunchwell Snacks is a mid-sized US snack brand sold mainly through grocery and mass retailers. This document is the starting point for anyone new to the account.

## Product lines
Crunchwell Original is the flagship potato chip and makes up about 60% of revenue. Leaf Crisps is a vegetable-based crisp launched two years ago and aimed at younger, health-curious buyers. Lunchbox Bites is a multipack of small portions sold to parents for school lunches.

## Audiences
Two audiences matter most. Gen Z Snackers are 18 to 26, discover products on TikTok and buy mostly at convenience and campus stores. Value Shoppers are price-conscious households who buy multipacks, plan their shop around promotions and read shelf prices closely.

## Media budget
The FY2026 media budget is $52M. Roughly 34% goes to TV and connected TV, 22% to social and TikTok, 18% to retail media, 12% to paid search, 8% to YouTube and 6% to other channels. Four agencies share the work, and the agency scorecard explains who does what.

## Goals for FY2026
Grow Leaf Crisps revenue by 20%, hold Original volume through a planned price increase, and prove which channels earn their budget. The marketing mix model readout and the two half-year reviews track progress against these goals.
