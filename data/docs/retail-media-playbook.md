# Retail media playbook

## What retail media is
Retail media means advertising inside a retailer's own website, app or in-store screens. Crunchwell buys it through Parallax Digital.

## Why it matters
The retailer knows who bought what, so the team can see whether an ad led to a purchase. That is rare in marketing and is why the marketing mix model rated retail media highest for the next dollar of spend.

## How we use it
Target households that previously bought snacks, lapsed buyers and shoppers with an open list. Lead with the price and pack size. Always run a holdout group that sees no ads, so the lift can be measured.

## Watch-outs
Prices are rising as brands crowd in. Track the cost per thousand impressions every month and pause placements that stop paying back. Different retailers measure results differently, so compare lift within a retailer, not across them.

## Ownership
Parallax Digital runs the buying. A Crunchwell analyst reviews the lift reports each month.
