# Brand guidelines for advertising

## Voice
Crunchwell sounds playful, confident and a little cheeky. We talk like a friend who loves snacks, not like a lecturer.

## Claims
Never make health claims. Do not say a product is healthy, good for you or diet friendly. For Leaf Crisps, describe what it is made of, not what it does for the body. Any statement about ingredients must match the pack.

## Price and offers
Show the price only when it is a real shelf price. Offers need the retailer's approved wording and an end date.

## Creators
Creators must disclose they are paid and may only say things they have tried. They may not read health claims, even if they believe them.

## Approvals
All new creative goes through the brand team. Agencies cannot publish without written approval, and the legal team reviews any new claim.
