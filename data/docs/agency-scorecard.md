# Agency scorecard

Crunchwell works with four agencies, each chosen for one strength.

## Northbeam Media
Strong in video, on YouTube and connected TV. Planned Crunch Time. Fee is 7% of spend. Strong on reach and planning, slower to react to weak results. Score 8 out of 10.

## Parallax Digital
Specialises in retail media. Planned Lunchbox Heroes and has the deepest relationships with retailer ad teams. Fee is 8% of spend. Excellent measurement. Score 9 out of 10.

## Ledger & Co
Specialises in paid search. Manages Snack Smart. Fee is 6% of spend. Reports are clear but they tend to optimise for reported return, which overstates true value. Score 7 out of 10.

## Tidewater Creative
Specialises in TikTok and social. Ran Leaf Local and the creator retainers. Fee is 10% of spend because creator management is labour heavy. Fast and creative. Score 9 out of 10.

## Overall
Parallax Digital and Tidewater Creative earned the budget increases after the marketing mix model readout.
