# Third quarter 2026 performance review

## Headline
Net revenue was up 3.4% year on year. Lunchbox Bites had its best quarter ever thanks to the back-to-school campaign, and Original volume stabilised for the first time since the price increase.

## Back to school
Lunchbox Heroes ran from July to September, led by the retail media agency. It returned 2.2 dollars per dollar and reached households in the weeks they were building shopping lists. It was the first campaign to bring Value Shoppers back above their pre-increase volume.

## Social
Tidewater Creative kept the TikTok creators running between campaigns. Return held near 2.5, which supports the idea that always-on creator work beats short bursts for the Gen Z audience.

## Budget moves
After the marketing mix model readout we moved 2.5 million dollars out of paid search and connected TV and into retail media and TikTok. Early results look positive but the quarter is too short to call it proven.

## Risks
Retail media costs are rising because every competitor is making the same move. We should watch cost per thousand impressions monthly.
