# Campaign: Lunchbox Heroes

## Brief
Lunchbox Heroes was the back-to-school push for Lunchbox Bites, running July through September. The target was Value Shoppers, parents watching their grocery budget. Parallax Digital planned it almost entirely inside retailer media networks.

## Results
Spend was 7.4 million dollars and it returned 2.2 dollars per dollar. Because the ads ran on retailer sites and apps, the team could see purchases at the shelf and compare households who saw the ad with those who did not. Exposed households bought 14% more multipacks.

## Learnings
Offers mattered more than creative. Ads showing a multipack price outperformed ads about nutrition. The campaign was also the first to recover Value Shoppers after the April price rise. The one issue was cost: retailer ad prices rose through August as more brands bid for the same placements.
