# Campaign: Crunch Time

## Brief
Crunch Time ran from January to March to defend Crunchwell Original during the quietest part of the year for snacks. The big idea was a loud, funny television spot built around the sound of the first bite. Northbeam Media planned and bought the campaign across connected TV and YouTube.

## Results
Spend was 9.5 million dollars. It reached 41 million people and returned 1.9 dollars for every dollar spent. Awareness of the product rose 12 points. The campaign did not move sales much among younger buyers.

## Learnings
The spot wore out after about six weeks, so we swapped in a shorter cut and saw a small recovery. Most of the effect came from the connected TV buy. The YouTube part was cheaper per view but did less. The agency recommended fewer, longer flights next time.
