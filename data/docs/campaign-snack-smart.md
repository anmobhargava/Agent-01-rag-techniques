# Campaign: Snack Smart

## Brief
Snack Smart is the always-on paid search programme that runs all year. It bids on snack and brand searches so Crunchwell appears when someone is ready to buy. Ledger & Co manages the account.

## Results
Annual spend was 5.1 million dollars and the reported return was 3.1 dollars per dollar, the highest in the portfolio. However, a large share of the people who clicked would likely have bought anyway, so the real incremental return is lower than reported.

## Learnings
Returns flatten as monthly spend rises. A test in May where spend was cut by a third lost very little revenue. The sensible move is to hold search steady and keep it for brand protection and high-intent terms, not to chase volume. This is the main example behind the case that search is saturated.
