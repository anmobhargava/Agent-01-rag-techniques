# Memo: April 2026 price increase

## Decision
Crunchwell raised shelf prices on Original by 6% on 1 April to cover higher potato and packaging costs. Leaf Crisps and Lunchbox Bites were not changed.

## Expected effect
The team expected a small volume drop and planned to protect margin. Retailers were told six weeks ahead and the sales team prepared promotions to soften the change.

## Actual effect
Original volume fell 2.2% across the first half. The drop was concentrated in Value Shoppers, down 3.8%, while Gen Z Snackers were flat. Revenue still rose because the higher price outweighed the lost volume.

## Response
Early promotions were too generic and did not win back price-sensitive households. From July, the team moved offers into retailer media through the Lunchbox Heroes campaign. By the third quarter Original volume had stopped declining.

## Lesson
Next time, test the price move with each audience before launch and prepare targeted offers in advance.
