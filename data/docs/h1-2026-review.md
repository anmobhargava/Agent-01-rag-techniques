# First half 2026 performance review

## Headline
Net revenue grew 4.1% year on year in the first half. Growth came mostly from Leaf Crisps, which was up 17%. Original volume fell 2.2% after the April price increase.

## Channel performance
Paid search delivered the best reported return at 3.1 dollars of revenue for every dollar spent, but most of that revenue was demand that already existed. Connected TV reached 41 million people during Crunch Time and returned 1.9. TikTok creators drove the Leaf Local launch and returned 2.6. Retail media returned 2.3 and was the only channel where we could see sales at the exact shelf.

## What worked
Leaf Local showed that creator content can move a younger buyer from first look to purchase inside two weeks. Crunch Time showed that a heavy television flight lifts awareness well but fades within a month once it stops.

## What did not
The price increase hurt Value Shoppers more than expected. The first promotions after April did not win them back. Search spend kept climbing even though returns stopped improving above a certain monthly level.

## Open questions
Is search saturated, and how much of the television effect is lasting? The marketing mix model readout is meant to answer both.
