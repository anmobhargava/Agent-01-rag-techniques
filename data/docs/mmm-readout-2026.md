# Marketing mix model readout, 2026

## What the model does
The model estimates how much each channel adds to sales after accounting for seasonality, price and promotions. It handles two things that matter: how long an ad keeps working after it airs, and how returns shrink as spend grows.

## Carryover
Television effects last the longest, with a half-life of about three weeks. TikTok effects fade fastest, with a half-life of about five days. Retail media effects are short but come with the clearest link to purchases.

## Saturation
Paid search is saturated. Above roughly 450 thousand dollars a month the extra return is close to zero. Connected TV is nearing its limit. Retail media and TikTok still have room.

## Return by channel
Marginal return on the next dollar was highest in retail media, then TikTok, then connected TV, then paid search. Intervals are wide for YouTube because the channel moved little during the year.

## Recommendation
Move 2.5 million dollars from paid search and connected TV into retail media and TikTok. Expect a revenue gain of roughly 3 to 5 percent on those dollars, and re-fit the model after one quarter. The estimates are directional, not exact.
