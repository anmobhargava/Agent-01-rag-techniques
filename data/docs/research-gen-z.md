# Consumer research: Gen Z Snackers

## Who they are
People aged 18 to 26, many of them students or early in their careers. They snack throughout the day, often while studying, gaming or travelling.

## How they find products
Discovery happens on TikTok and through friends. They trust creators who show real use and distrust polished advertising. A creator trying a snack on camera is more persuasive to this group than a television spot.

## What they buy
They buy single bags at convenience stores, campus shops and petrol stations. Price matters, but flavour novelty and an easy-to-share image matter more. Vegetable-based and lower-oil options attract them because they feel better about snacking.

## Price sensitivity
After the April price increase, Gen Z volumes stayed flat. Their bags are small, so the extra cost per purchase was only a few cents and rarely noticed.

## Implication
Keep creators on retainer, favour authentic demonstrations and avoid long television flights for this audience.
